# Product overview

## The idea

FixIn10 is an on-demand home-services app for Indian cities. You notice something broken, open the app, pick a trade (electrician, carpenter or plumber), and FixIn10 matches you with a verified nearby professional in about 10 minutes. You watch the pro arrive live, the job starts with an OTP code, the app meters the work by the hour, and you pay only for the time actually spent. Rate the pro, tip if you like, and the receipt lands in your bookings.

## The problem

- **Trust:** finding a reliable pro is word of mouth, and quality varies wildly.
- **Price anxiety:** no one knows what a repair should cost, so haggling and over-charging are the norm.
- **Speed:** small fixes stall for weeks because booking anything feels like a project.

## Who it is for

- **Customers:** renters and families in metros who want repairs done without haggling. Urban, mobile-first, comfortable with UPI.
- **Pros:** electricians, carpenters and plumbers who want steady work, clear job details and reliable payouts.

## How it works (customer journey)

1. **Sign in:** phone + OTP. Set your address once.
2. **Pick a trade and task:** browse services with clear hourly prices.
3. **Schedule:** Now (fastest) or a slot today, or up to a week ahead.
4. **Review and pay:** estimate shown up front. Payment method selected now, collected later.
5. **Match:** three nearby pros are pinged; the first to accept wins. Typical match under 10 minutes.
6. **Track:** live map, ETA, call and chat, SOS.
7. **Start with a code:** the pro enters a 4-digit code to start; the hourly meter begins.
8. **Work and bill:** the meter runs during the job; the final bill covers actual hours (15-minute steps), visit fee, coupons and tip.
9. **Rate and return:** review, receipt, rebook. Wallet, Plus membership and referrals keep people coming back.

## Service and pricing model

- **Hourly rate per trade (sample):** electrician from ₹249/hr, carpenter from ₹229/hr, plumber from ₹219/hr.
- **Visit fee:** ₹49 flat (₹0 for Plus members).
- **Billing:** 15-minute steps, only time actually worked. The estimate shown before booking is an upper-bound guide.
- **Coupons:** e.g. FIRST50 (50% off up to ₹50 on the first booking).
- **Tips:** optional, after the job.
- **FixIn10 Plus:** ₹99/month or ₹799/year. Zero visit fee, priority matching, 5% back, two free cancellations a month.
- **Referrals:** ₹100 for the inviter and ₹100 for the friend after their first completed booking.
- **Wallet:** balance, refunds and rewards live here.

## Trust and safety

- Verified pros: KYC plus ratings and job counts on every profile.
- OTP start code: no code, no start, no charge.
- Live tracking with share-ETA, in-app chat, 24x7 support and an SOS action.
- 15-day service warranty on fixes; free cancellation up to 1 hour before the slot.

## Market context (why this can win)

Well-known India players: **Urban Company** (broad coverage, trusted brand, polished UX, but heavy catalogue and inspection-based pricing), **Housejoy** (packages and promotions, quality perception varies), **Sulekha / Justdial** (huge listing marketplaces with weaker in-app experience and quality control).

The openings FixIn10 targets:

- A real **10-minute match promise** for small-ticket fixes, with hyperlocal "Instant vs Schedule" logic.
- **Price transparency:** hourly rates visible up front, billed for actual time, no haggling.
- **A lightweight, fast app** that works well on mid-range Android phones and low bandwidth.
- **Trust by design:** OTP start, live tracking, warranties, human support.

## Success signals to watch (proposed)

- Match time: median under 10 minutes in dense zones.
- Job completion rate, and rebooking rate within 60 days.
- Pro acceptance rate and earnings stability.
- Share of bookings using coupons / Plus / referrals (the loyalty loops).
