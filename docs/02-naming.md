# Naming: why FixIn10

**Chosen name: FixIn10.** Tagline: "Home repairs, fixed in minutes."

## Why it works

- The promise is the name: a fix, in ten minutes.
- Short, easy to say in English and understandable across India; it keeps the "10" from the project's original idea name (tenmin-service).
- Works as a wordmark ("FixIn10") and as speech ("fix in ten").
- Distinct in the home-services space.

## Research behind the choice

Quick scan (app stores plus web search, October 2026):

**Dropped: occupied in the same category**

- **FixKar** - an active service-booking platform (fixkarr.com) and a Play Store app named FixKar for handyman and construction services.
- **FixMitro** - "Fix Mitro Home Service App" already on Google Play for home services (electrician, plumber and more).
- **Fixo** - multiple home-services apps exist (a home services app in India, Fixo Handyman, FIXO in Portugal).

**Dropped: crowded family**

- **TezFix / Tez- prefix** - the Tez family is busy in India: TezFood (delivery), TezClean (cleaning staff), Tez Health (home healthcare); Google's earlier India payments app was also named Tez.

**Finalists**

- **FixIn10** - no direct app collisions found; the promise is embedded; chosen.
- **TenMin** - clean but generic and hard to protect; bench alternate.
- **TezFix** - punchy but sits in a crowded family; bench alternate.
